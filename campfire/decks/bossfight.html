Boss Fight

1:17 · Everybody · Seven bosses, each with a weakness

Level eight: the boss fight. Every risk is a boss, and every boss has a weakness. Seven of them. Nobody dies, but some of them hit hard.
Boss one: the Hallucinator. It attacks with confident errors, the render that ignores gravity. Its weakness: verify, ask for sources, and test what it built.
Boss two: the Leaker. It attacks with confidential data in the wrong chat. Its weakness: a business plan, the client NDA first, anonymise, and ask before pasting.
Boss three: the Key Goblin, who loves passwords and keys in code. Its weakness: a vault, never in files. Let an AI audit your repos, and rotate whatever it finds.

Bosses 4 and 5

The Licence Lich attacks with fonts, images, code and AI output: own it or license it, check the tool terms and the client contract, and label AI content where required. The Orphan Golem is the tool nobody maintains: every tool has an owner, and the docs live in the repo.

Bosses 6 and 7

The Lock-in Dragon wants one vendor for everything. Pull requests and open formats beat it, so any AI can take the next shift. And the Brain Drain: people stop thinking. AI amplifies taste; keep the taste human.
