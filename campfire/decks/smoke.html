06

Smoke signals

Risks and sensible use. Rules, not worries.

Station six: smoke signals. Honest and practical, no fear-mongering. Seven signals, and for each one what to do. You should leave with rules, not worries.

06 · Smoke 1 and 2

Confidential data

Client briefs, NDAs, personal data in consumer chats.

Do: business plans only. Anonymise. Ask before pasting.

Secrets

API keys and passwords end up in code, even in private repos.

Do: keys in a vault or env, never in files. Rotate when exposed.

First, confidential data: client briefs, NDAs and personal data don't belong in consumer chats. Use business plans, anonymise, and ask before pasting. Second, secrets: keys and passwords end up in code, even in private repos. Keep them in a vault, never in files, and rotate them when exposed.

06 · Smoke 3 and 4

Confident errors

It sounds right. It's wrong.

Do: verify facts, test behaviour, ask for sources.

Shipped insecurity

Logins, databases, prompt injection.

Do: row-level security, reviews. Never trust outside text.

Third, confident errors: remember the next-word machine, it sounds right and can be wrong. Verify facts, test behaviour, ask for sources. Fourth, shipped insecurity: logins, databases and prompt injection. Use row-level security and reviews, and never trust text that comes from outside.

06 · Smoke 5 and 6

Rights

Fonts, images, code licences, real people's photos.

Do: use what you own or what's licensed. Credit sources.

Orphan tools

Who fixes it in six months?

Do: every tool has an owner. Docs live in the repo.

Fifth, rights: fonts, images, code licences and real people's photos. Use what you own or what's licensed, and credit your sources. Sixth, orphan tools: who fixes it in six months? Every tool needs an owner, and the docs live in the repo.
