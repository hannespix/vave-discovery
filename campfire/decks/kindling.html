03 · The prompt recipe

Six lines, and the AI stops guessing

  • Who is it for

    The person who will use it.

  • Goal

    What should be possible afterwards.

  • Context

    What the AI can't know.

  • Constraints

    One file, offline, no logins.

  • Done when

    How you will check it.

  • Look & feel

    Your taste, in words.

Here's the recipe: who is it for, the goal, the context, the constraints, when it's done, and the look and feel. Six lines, and the AI stops guessing. The Prompt Forge module lets us build one together.
