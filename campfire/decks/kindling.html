Level 4 · The prompt recipe

Six lines, and the AI stops guessing

  • Who

    The person who will use it.

  • Goal

    What should be possible afterwards.

  • Context

    What the AI can't know.

  • Constraints

    One file, offline, no logins.

  • Done when

    How you will check it.

  • Look & feel

    Your taste, in words.

The prompt recipe: who, goal, context, constraints, done when, and look and feel. It's a good client brief, written for a machine. The Prompt Forge module lets us build one together.
